# Belgian-Style Blonde Ale

- **Color:** Straw to light amber
- **Clarity:** Chill haze is acceptable at low temperatures
- **Perceived Malt Aroma & Flavor:** Low to medium.
- **Perceived Hop Aroma & Flavor:** Very low to medium. Noble-type hops are commonly used.
- **Perceived bitterness:** Very low to medium-low
- **Fermentation Characteristics:** Medium to medium-high fruity esters are balanced with low to medium malt attributes. Low level yeast-derived phenolic spiciness may be present. Diacetyl and acidic character should not be present.
- **Body:** Low to medium

## Vital Statistics:

- OG (°Plato): 1.054 - 1.068 (13.3 - 16.6 °Plato)
- FG (°Plato): 1.010 - 1.014 (2.6 - 3.6 °Plato)
- IBUs: 15 - 40
- SRM: 2 - 7
- ABV: 6.3 - 7.9%
