# M3. SPICED MEAD

_Spiced Mead includes mead with some form of added spice, herb, or vegetable (SHV). The general term **spice** is defined as in category 30 Spiced Beer in the 2021 BJCP Beer Style Guidelines, including the distinction between specific usages of **spice**, **herb**, and **vegetable**. Table salt at a noticeable level is considered a spice. Hopped meads are best entered in the Experimental Mead style. The category is divided into three styles based on the specific ingredients used; read the style preambles for details._

_Category M1 describes the base character expected from dry, semi-sweet, and sweet meads._
