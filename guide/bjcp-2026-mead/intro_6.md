# Overall Impression

- Mead should express an enjoyable balance of honey flavors, sweetness, acidity, tannins, and alcohol.
- Any additional ingredients should likewise be well-integrated into the final presentation, not seem like an add-on or afterthought.
- Mead should be well-fermented and conditioned, presenting as a coherent fermented beverage not a raw, honey-based cocktail.
- Flavors should be a pure, fresh, and natural expression of the declared ingredients. Fermentation and conditioning can add complexity.
