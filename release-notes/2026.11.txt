Beer Style Guidelines 2026.11

Beer Style Guidelines 2026.11 - iOS, iPadOS, & macOS
==================================

Ready for iPhone Duo, and steadier everywhere.

• Made for iPhone Duo. Reading text, empty-screen messages and the favorites confirmation now stay clear of the fold, whether the phone is open like a book or bent like a tabletop.
• Clearer titles on iPhone Duo. When a guide's full name won't fit, the title switches to its short name.
• More reliable guide switching. Changing guides, or opening a style from Handoff or a link, no longer leaves a section from the previous guide on screen.
• Easier reading in About and the Tip Jar. The About heading no longer gets cut off, and both screens now scroll to show all their text.
• On Mac, the favorites confirmation no longer covers the color scale.

This version requires iOS 26 or later.
