Beer Style Guidelines 2026.9 - iOS, iPadOS, & macOS
==================================

A fresh look, and a better read.

• Redesigned for Liquid Glass. Toolbars, the favorites confirmation and the tip jar all match the new look of iOS 26.
• Easier reading on iPad. Style descriptions now keep a comfortable line length instead of stretching across the whole screen.
• Better search. Results stay in guide order, chapter by chapter, and the list on iPad returns to its chapters as soon as you clear the search.
• Clearer scrolling. Section headers no longer collide with the navigation bar as you scroll.
• Accessibility improvements. VoiceOver announces when you add or remove a favorite, colors hold up better in Dark Mode and with Increase Contrast, and the app respects Reduce Motion.
• A refreshed app icon.

This version requires iOS 26 or later.
