Beer Style Guidelines 2026.12

Beer Style Guidelines 2026.12 - iOS, iPadOS, & macOS
==================================

Two new guides, and a careful proofread of the rest.

• New: BJCP 2026 Mead Style Guidelines, with the reorganized fruit, spice and experimental mead categories. It sits next to the 2015 mead guide.
• New: Brewers Association 2026 Beer Style Guidelines.
• Corrected vital statistics. Gravity and alcohol ranges that didn't match the published guidelines now do, in BJCP 2015 Cider and the Brewers Association 2016, 2020 and 2025 guides.
• BJCP 2015 Smoked Beer fixed. 32A Classic Style Smoked Beer and 32B Specialty Smoked Beer each show their own description again.
• Typo fixes throughout. Style names and text in BJCP 2015 and 2021, the BJCP cider and mead guides, and the Brewers Association 2016–2025 guides now match the published guidelines.

This version requires iOS/macOS 26 or later.
